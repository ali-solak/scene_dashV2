Summary, mean of three:
  Query1.each                     7.0 -> 4.7 ns/entity  (-34%)

allocation the extension type removes. The 0.7.0 changelog previously quoted
5.0 -> 3.8 ns/entity from an unsaved run; it now cites these numbers.
