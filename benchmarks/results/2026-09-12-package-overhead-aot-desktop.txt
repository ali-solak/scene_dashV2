The parked-query benchmark measures steady-state queries after warmup. A
newly parked part invalidates the cache and still requires a scan on the
next typed use. The event cases isolate reader operations, excluding channel
setup/emission and system dispatch. drain().isNotEmpty reproduces the old
consumeAny cursor operation; consume() is the new operation. Setup is outside
the timer. Very short operations include Stopwatch overhead and cache noise.
There was a small increase in the no-parked query case (82 to 88 ns); the
improvement concerns repeated scans of an unchanged backlog.
